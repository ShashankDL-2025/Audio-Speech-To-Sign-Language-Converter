About Speak To 3D-Sign Language Translator

The Speak To 3D-Sign Language Translator is an innovative web application designed to promote inclusive communication for the deaf and hard-of-hearing community. It intelligently converts spoken or typed words into Indian Sign Language (ISL) animations, ensuring that users can understand and interact seamlessly with others. This project integrates Natural Language Processing (NLP) and 3D animated sign rendering to provide real-time visual output. Our goal is to enhance accessibility, bridge communication barriers, and empower individuals through technology-driven solutions. We believe in using AI for social good creating tools that not only showcase innovation but also make a real difference in people’s lives.
